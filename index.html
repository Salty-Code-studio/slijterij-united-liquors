Good to know

Questions, answered.

Quick answers to what people ask us most.

When are you open?

Every day, 11:00–22:00 — weekends included.

Is delivery more expensive than the shop?

Yes — delivery runs through Thuisbezorgd, which adds platform and courier costs. Picking up in the shop is always cheapest.

Do you have a specific bottle?

Probably! Our range changes weekly, so send us the name or a photo on WhatsApp and we'll check the shelf.

How do I get there from Centraal?

Take the Westside exit and walk about two minutes to Hekelveld 4 — see the map above.

How can I pay?

Cash, PIN and contactless in the shop; iDEAL through Thuisbezorgd.

Do I need ID?

If you look under 25, yes. No alcohol under 18 — in the shop and on delivery.

Still wondering? Message the owners, we reply fast.
