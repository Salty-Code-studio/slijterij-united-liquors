Finding us

Two minutes from Centraal.

  1. Leave Amsterdam Centraal by the Westside exit (Westzijde).
  2. Cross the Prins Hendrikkade and walk up the Martelaarsgracht to the Hekelveld.
  3. Hekelveld 4: look for the round United Liquors sign.
