Delivery & prices

Delivery?
Honestly, here's the catch…

Delivery in central Amsterdam, every day 11:00–22:00 through Thuisbezorgd. Delivery prices are higher than our shop prices — we'd rather you hear it from us.

  • Our lowest prices — the ones people review us for

Most convenient

Delivered

  • Prices higher than in the shop
  • Thuisbezorgd delivery & service fees on top
  • Central Amsterdam · daily 11:00–22:00
  • 18+ — ID is checked at the door

Why the difference? Ordering apps charge the shop a commission on every bottle, and couriers cost money. Rather than raise prices for everyone, we keep shop prices low and the delivery price covers the delivery.
