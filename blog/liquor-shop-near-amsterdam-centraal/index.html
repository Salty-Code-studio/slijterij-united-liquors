The best liquor shop near Amsterdam Centraal (without tourist prices)

Two minutes from the station, honest prices and a huge range. Here's how to find us.

  • Updated 30 May 2026
  • 4 min read
  • Local guide

Step out of Amsterdam Central Station and you're surrounded by shops that know exactly one thing about you: you're a visitor, and you'll probably pay whatever's on the label. Drinks near the station can carry a steep "tourist tax." It doesn't have to be that way.

Slijterij United Liquors sits at Hekelveld 4, about a two-minute walk from Centraal — close enough to be convenient, far enough to be priced fairly. Locals and visitors shop here for the same reason: a big selection, honest prices, and someone behind the counter who'll actually point you to the right bottle.

Where we are & how to find us

From Centraal, take the Westside exit (Westzijde) and walk straight for roughly two minutes — you'll reach Hekelveld 4, 1012 SN Amsterdam. If you'd rather follow the map, see the exact location and get directions here.

We're open every day, 11:00–22:00 — including weekends and holidays. Late enough for a last-minute bottle on the way to dinner, early enough for an afternoon pick-up.

What "no tourist prices" actually means

It's simple: everything is price-labelled, and the price is the price — the same one a local pays. No marked-up minibottles, no mystery surcharge for asking a question. We'd rather you come back than overpay once.

What you'll find inside

It's a proper bottle shop, not a corner fridge. Among the shelves:

  • Whisky — from everyday Scotch and Irish to Japanese and the occasional collectible.
  • Vodka, gin, rum, tequila — the classics plus a few you won't have tried.
  • Cognac, brandy & Dutch jenever — including local specialties.
  • Absinthe, ouzo, sambuca & other anise spirits — a surprisingly deep corner.
  • Wines, champagne & bubbles, beer, and all the mixers, snacks and party cups to go with them.

Want the full overview? Here's everything we stock. Looking for one specific bottle? Call us on +31 20 785 1344 — odds are it's on the shelf, and if not we'll point you to who has it.

Can't make it to the shop?

We also deliver across central Amsterdam. If you're at a hotel or apartment near the centre, it's often easier to have it brought over — see how alcohol delivery in Amsterdam works, or order on Thuisbezorgd.

Good to know (18+)

The legal age to buy alcohol in the Netherlands is 18, in store and on delivery — bring ID. We card when in doubt; it's the law and we'd rather get it right.

It's your choice. We just make sure it's a fair one.
