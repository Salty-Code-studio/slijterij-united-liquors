Buying alcohol

Where can I buy alcohol in Amsterdam?

Beer and wine under 15% are sold in supermarkets. Spirits, and anything else from 15% up, are only sold in a slijterij: a licensed liquor store. We're one of them, at Hekelveld 4, two minutes from Centraal.

Is there a liquor store near Amsterdam Centraal?

Yes. Take the Westside exit (Westzijde), cross the Prins Hendrikkade and walk up the Martelaarsgracht to the Hekelveld. About two minutes on foot. Route and opening hours.

Can supermarkets in the Netherlands sell spirits?

No. Supermarkets can only sell drinks under 15% alcohol. Whisky, gin, vodka, rum, jenever and liqueurs come from a slijterij.

What time do liquor stores close in Amsterdam?

It differs per shop. We're open every day from 11:00 to 22:00, weekends included.

Can I buy alcohol on a Sunday?

Yes. We're open on Sundays from 11:00 to 22:00, the same as every other day.

Can I buy alcohol late at night?

Until 22:00 at our shop. After that, you'll need a bar or café.

Do you have a specific bottle in stock?

Our stock changes every week, so we don't list it online. Send us the name or a photo on WhatsApp and we'll check the shelf for you, and put it aside if you like.

Age, ID and the rules

What is the legal drinking age in the Netherlands?

18, for every kind of alcohol: beer and wine included.

Do I need ID to buy alcohol?

If you look under 25, expect to be asked. A passport, ID card or driving licence works. No ID, no sale; it's the law, not us being difficult.

Can tourists buy alcohol in Amsterdam?

Of course. The same rules apply to everyone: 18+, and bring your passport or ID card.

Can I drink alcohol on the street in Amsterdam?

In many parts of the city centre, no: drinking in public is banned there and you can be fined. Enjoy your bottle at home, at your hotel or in a bar.

Can I buy alcohol for someone under 18?

No. Shops will refuse the sale if they think you're buying for someone under 18.

Delivery and payment

Can I get alcohol delivered in Amsterdam?

Yes, we deliver through Thuisbezorgd. Delivery prices are higher than in the shop, and the courier checks your ID at the door. How delivery works.

How can I pay?

In the shop: cash, PIN and contactless. Online orders through Thuisbezorgd: iDEAL.

Is it cheaper to buy in the shop?

Yes. Shop prices are lower than delivery prices, and you can ask us for advice while you're there.

Dutch drinks and gifts

What is jenever?

The Dutch original and the ancestor of gin, made with juniper and malt wine. 'Jonge' and 'oude' refer to the recipe, not the age: jonge is lighter, oude is maltier.

What's a good Dutch drink to take home as a souvenir?

A bottle of jenever is the classic: Bols, Hartevelt or Rutte. Ask us and we'll help you pick one for your budget.

Is absinthe legal in the Netherlands?

Yes, absinthe is legal and we have a proper absinthe shelf. Read our absinthe guide.

Can you help me with drinks for a party?

Yes. Tell us how many people and your budget on WhatsApp, and we'll suggest what to get. Ice and mixers are in the shop too.

Taking bottles home

Can I take alcohol home on the plane?

Usually yes, in your checked luggage. Hand luggage has liquid limits, and how much you can bring into your country depends on its customs rules, so check with your airline and destination before you fly.
